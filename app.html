How the score works

Each open finding adds weight by severity (critical 35%, high 20%, medium 10%, low 3%, info 0) times confidence (high ×1, medium ×0.75, low ×0.5); weights combine as 100 × (1 − Π(1 − w)). It summarises these findings only. It is not a probability of being hacked, and 0 does not mean secure.
